How the final quote is built

Route. Vehicle. Trip type. Additions.

1. Route

Distance between pickup and destination, any planned stops and whether the return leg is included.

2. Vehicle

Sedan, SUV or premium category, since each carries a different per-kilometre rate and capacity.

3. Trip type

One way, round trip, local or airport transfer — each has its own fare structure and timing rules.

4. Additions

Tolls, parking, permits, driver allowance, night halt and minimum kilometre conditions.

Want a figure for your journey? Send the route, travel date, passenger count and preferred vehicle category through the booking enquiry form. You will receive a written quote that lists each component that applies — no hidden additions after the fact.

Tariff questions

Understanding your quote.

Your fare is calculated transparently based on the actual distance travelled, vehicle category (Sedan, SUV or Premium), journey type (one-way drop or round trip), and applicable highway tolls or state permits. For one-way drops, you only pay for the one-way distance with no return fare charges.

A driver allowance is a daily amount that covers the driver's food, stay and time away from home on longer journeys. It is common on outstation and multi-day round trips and is usually quoted separately from the per-kilometre rate.

They are included only when the quote says so. On most outstation and airport journeys tolls and parking are either estimated in the total or billed as actuals with receipts. Ask for an all-inclusive quote if you prefer one fixed figure.

Many one way and outstation bookings carry a minimum running condition — for example a minimum number of kilometres per day, or per trip. If your journey is shorter than the minimum, the minimum applies. The applicable condition for your route is confirmed in the quote.

Permits are required for some interstate journeys and a few restricted routes. Where a permit is needed, the charge is shown as a separate line in the quote rather than hidden inside the kilometre rate.

A one way drop is priced on the distance covered in a single direction. A round trip is priced on the full running distance plus driver allowance, and may include a night halt charge for multi-day journeys. Waiting time policies are confirmed with the quote.
