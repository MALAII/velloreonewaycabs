What we arrange

Six services. One clear way of travelling.

From a single one way drop to a week-long family circuit, each journey is planned around the same principles: the right vehicle, a route that makes sense and a fare you understood before you left.

The services

Choose the journey closest to your plan.

Not sure which one fits? Describe the trip in a sentence — the team will suggest the category that works best for your passengers, luggage and timing.

Service 01

One Way Drop Taxi

Pay for one direction only. A one way drop is the right choice when you are relocating, catching a flight, attending a function or simply not returning with the same vehicle.

Benefits

  • Single-direction travel where a return leg is not needed and the fare is based on the distance covered.
  • Vehicle category matched to passengers and luggage
  • Fare explained in writing before the journey is confirmed

Suitable for

  • Chennai, Bengaluru, Tirupati and nearby city drops
  • Airport and railway station transfers
  • Single-direction business travel

Round Trip Taxi

The vehicle stays with you for the itinerary. Round trips suit same-day returns, family functions, temple visits and multi-day journeys where you would rather not re-book a cab at every stop.

Benefits

  • Out-and-back journeys where your travel time, waiting periods and route plan are agreed in advance.
  • Vehicle category matched to passengers and luggage
  • Fare explained in writing before the journey is confirmed

Suitable for

  • Same-day return journeys
  • Multi-day family itineraries
  • Pilgrimage and temple circuits

Service questions

Before you choose a service.

A one way drop covers a single direction, so you pay for the journey you actually need. A round trip reserves the vehicle for the outbound and return legs together, including any waiting time between them. Round trips usually make sense when you are returning the same day or keeping the car with you across multiple stops.

Yes. Local bookings can be arranged on an hourly or full-day basis, subject to vehicle availability. Share the places you need to visit and the time you expect to finish, and the team will suggest a suitable arrangement.

Flight numbers are recorded with the booking so the pickup can be monitored. Waiting policies and any parking charges for airport pickups are confirmed in the quote, since they vary by airport and terminal.

Interstate journeys can be requested, but they depend on permit requirements, vehicle availability and the route. Share the details early and the team will confirm whether the trip can be arranged and what the permit charges would be.

Inform the team as soon as possible with the revised date, time or destination. Changes are accommodated subject to vehicle availability, and any change in distance, permits or waiting time may affect the fare.

For a family of four or more with luggage, an SUV or larger vehicle is usually the better fit. For a same-day return with multiple stops, a round trip with a reserved vehicle tends to work better than separate one way bookings.
