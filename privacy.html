Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information you enter on this website. It is written in plain language because a privacy notice is only useful if it can be understood.

What this website collects

This site has two interactive forms: the booking enquiry form on the home page and the journey enquiry form on the contact page. Both forms ask for the details needed to plan a cab journey — typically your name, mobile number, pickup and drop locations, travel date, journey type, passenger count and preferred vehicle category.

Forms on this site run entirely in your browser. Until a WhatsApp booking number or a server-side form handler is configured, the information you type is not transmitted anywhere. It is formatted on your device and you are offered the option to copy it to your clipboard.

How enquiry details are used

Once a booking channel is connected, the details you submit will be used only to:

  • Check vehicle availability for your route and travel date
  • Prepare and share a fare quote for your journey
  • Contact you about the journey you have asked about
  • Keep a record of the booking for operational purposes

WhatsApp enquiries

If you choose to send a booking enquiry through WhatsApp, the message is delivered through WhatsApp's own platform and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy terms. The website does not receive a copy of that conversation.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added in future, this page will be updated before any tracking is enabled, and the tooling will be configured to avoid collecting personal information from enquiry forms.

Third-party resources

Fonts are self-hosted on this website rather than loaded from a third-party font service. Photographs are stored locally on the site. If a map embed or a messaging integration is added later, that section will identify the provider.

Data retention

Journey enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond to your request and to maintain records of completed journeys. You can ask for your enquiry details to be removed by contacting the business through the contact page.

Your choices

You can ask what enquiry information is held about you, request a correction, or request deletion. Because the public contact channels for this business are still being finalised, those details are not listed here yet — they will be published on the contact page.

Changes to this policy

If our service policies or booking systems are updated, this page will be updated and the revision date at the top will change.
