Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 5 November 2026

These terms describe how journey enquiries and bookings are handled by Vellore One Way Cabs. They apply to enquiries made through this website and to journeys arranged as a result.

1. Enquiries are not bookings

Submitting a form or sending a WhatsApp message starts a conversation. A journey is confirmed only when the business has checked vehicle availability, shared a fare, and received your acceptance — followed by a written confirmation of the pickup details.

2. Fares and inclusions

Fares are quoted for the specific route, vehicle category and travel date discussed. A quote states what is included. Tolls, parking charges, state permit fees, driver allowance, night halt charges and minimum kilometre conditions may apply and are confirmed before travel. Where a fare is estimated, actual charges for tolls or parking may replace the estimate.

3. Vehicle categories

Vehicle categories describe the class of vehicle, not a specific model. The vehicle assigned is confirmed at the time of booking, and seating or luggage capacity should be checked with the team before confirming if it matters for your journey.

4. Changes and cancellations

Changes to pickup point, destination, date or time should be reported as early as possible. Changes are subject to vehicle availability and may change the fare. Cancellation terms for confirmed bookings are shared with the booking confirmation, since they may depend on how close to the pickup time the cancellation is made.

5. Punctuality and waiting

Pickup times are planned with the route in mind. Waiting policies for airport pickups and multi-stop journeys are confirmed in the quote. Where a delay is caused by a flight or an event outside the traveller's control, inform the team as soon as possible so the driver can be updated.

6. Route and permit limitations

Some routes require permits, have restricted timings, or are affected by weather and road conditions — particularly hill roads. Where a route cannot be operated as planned, the team will discuss alternatives. The district and route information on this website describes destinations you can enquire about; it does not guarantee that every route is operated daily.

7. Traveller responsibilities

Travellers are responsible for the accuracy of the details provided, for complying with applicable law during the journey, and for ensuring passengers and luggage remain within the capacity of the assigned vehicle.

8. Website content

Photographs on this website are illustrative of vehicle categories and travel experiences; they are not photographs of a specific fleet. Passenger capacity, indicative per-kilometre rates and final all-inclusive fares are confirmed directly with our booking desk prior to departure.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the contact page. Public contact details will be published there as soon as they are confirmed.
